Isaac The Carpentopod

Isaac The Carpentopod is a walking robot that uses a special mathmatical linkage to walk. I made Isaac for a Hackclub event. The work I put into Isaac helped me attend a hackathon in Bogota, Colombia. In the project I used my CAD skills to design the robot in Onshape. I also used some prior electrical and arduino knowledge to wire up Isaacs motors and battires. I also learned more C++ to complete the project, as arduino runs on a slightly modified version of it. The projected was full of failure and challenges that taught me how to approch issues as somthing to solve, not a problem.
