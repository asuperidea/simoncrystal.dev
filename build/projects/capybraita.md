# Capybraita
Capybraita is a game two others and I made for a [Hackathon in Bogotá, Colombia](blog.html?post=macondo). The game was made in Unity over three days. In Capybraita, you play as a broke capybara trying to save up for a computer. You complete minigames around a Colombian neighborhood to earn mangoes, the game's currency. However, to stay alive, you must eat mangoes. The game wasn't great, we had a lot of bumps in the road. However we did ship it, and we definitely followed the hackathon theme: "make something ridiculous."
